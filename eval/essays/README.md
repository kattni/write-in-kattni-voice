# Generated essays from the write-in-kattni-voice eval

Every essay the eval generated between Aug 18 and Aug 20, 2026: 134 in total. Each one is labeled with its run, its scores and the judge's reasoning. The essays are unedited.

Most of them were recovered from the transcripts of the eval's background generation runs, because only runs 1 and 4 still have a results file. Times are US Eastern.

## Documents

- [writing your first test with pytest](01-writing-your-first-test-with-pytest.md): 30 essays
- [why good error messages are worth the effort](02-why-good-error-messages-are-worth-the-effort.md): 24 essays
- [the bug that took me three days to find](03-the-bug-that-took-me-three-days-to-find.md): 24 essays
- [stepping back from a project you maintained for years](04-stepping-back-from-a-project-you-maintained-for-years.md): 20 essays
- [the small tools that quietly changed how I work](05-the-small-tools-that-quietly-changed-how-i-work.md): 15 essays
- [Trial run topics](06-trial-run-topics.md): 21 essays on the value of learning in public; making your first open source contribution; why documentation deserves more care than it gets; a project that didn't go the way I planned; getting started with CircuitPython on a microcontroller

## Conditions

- **Skill**: `/write-in-kattni-voice` plus the task. Skill on.
- **Baseline with samples**: A standalone prompt carrying the same six writing samples, asking the model to match their style. Skill off.
- **Naive baseline**: A standalone prompt asking for "the writing voice of Kattni (a developer and technical writer)", with no samples. Skill off.

## Notes

- The skill version for each run comes from this repo's history and the skill files' timestamps. Runs 2–4 used edits that were saved before run 2 but committed later.
- Run 3 also made one more call that returned the usage-limit message instead of an essay. It is left out.
- Every essay was generated by `claude-sonnet-5` and judged by `claude-opus-5`.
- Headings inside essays were demoted three levels so they stay under their essay's heading.
